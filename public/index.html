5-Mark Standard Verified Reference
Q: What is Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL)?
Definition: Kirchhoff's Voltage Law states that the directed sum of electrical potential differences (voltages) around any closed loop or mesh in an electric circuit is equal to zero: ∑V = 0.

Physical Principle: KVL is a direct consequence of the conservation of energy in an electrostatic field. Work done in carrying a unit test charge in a closed electrostatic loop is identically zero.
Key Points: Applies to any lumped closed circuit loop · Direct expression of energy conservation · Sign convention: gains are positive, drops are negative.
Sources: Wikipedia Wikidata
